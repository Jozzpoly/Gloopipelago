# Gloopipelago — M4 Living World Amplification Contract

**Date:** 2026-09-10  
**Status:** **PREDECLARED CONTRACT / IMPLEMENTATION NOT YET AUTHORIZED BY RESULTS**

## Purpose

M4 exists because the current technically qualified product is not yet a compelling living evolution observatory. Owner evidence and a dedicated skeptical audit show that the current world is too spatially sparse, ecologically low-dimensional and phenotypically illegible.

M4 is not a generic feature milestone. Its falsifiable question is:

> Can a richer spatial/resource ecology make several inherited ways of life coexist long enough to become interesting to observe, and can the browser make those differences immediately legible without changing their causal semantics?

M4 is split into two independently qualified layers:

- **M4a — Ecological Mosaic**: intentional model/ecology change;
- **M4b — Phenotype Legibility**: presentation-only change.

They may be delivered to the Owner as one composite build, but evidence must keep their causal authority separate.

## Starting authority

M4 work is stacked on the exact machine-qualified M3+x40 lineage.

The current composite candidate remains unpromoted pending Owner evidence. M4 therefore must not rewrite history or pretend M3/x40 are already live `main` authority.

## Evidence motivating the contract

Canonical audit:

`evidence/living-world/LIVING_WORLD_INTEREST_AUDIT_2026-09-10.md`

Key baseline at 1,800 simulated seconds:

- median population ~71;
- median occupied world-grid fraction ~14.8%;
- median effective coarse strategies ~5.86;
- median surviving founder roots 2/34;
- median largest lineage share ~59.7%;
- strong convergence toward speed/sense upper regions and extreme diet specialization.

Existing-knob tuning could increase population or trait spread but did not create more enduring ecological structure.

The strongest exploratory evidence came from:

- eight-patch spatial mosaics;
- a scaled `1200x900` world/start budget;
- heterogeneous dense vs diffuse resource patches.

The paired heterogeneous-resource control produced repeatable local phenotype differences with essentially unchanged total population.

## M4a — Ecological Mosaic

### Intended semantic change

M4a may intentionally change:

- logical world geometry;
- founder/start-resource ecological budget;
- spatial resource-patch topology;
- patch-specific resource dispersion/regime;
- parameters needed to express those regimes explicitly and deterministically.

M4a must **not** change unrelated organism decision logic merely to force diversity.

### Leading candidate

The implementation campaign should begin from the following bounded candidate, subject to falsification before Owner delivery:

- logical world: **1200x900**;
- eight stable resource habitats distributed across the world;
- both food kinds represented across multiple habitats;
- paired **dense/predictable** and **diffuse/search-heavy** resource regimes;
- start population/resource budget scaled intentionally for the larger ecological area rather than inherited accidentally from 900x700;
- existing genome and basic foraging controller retained initially;
- existing fixed `1/60` simulation stepping retained;
- x40 scheduler semantics retained.

`1200x900` is a candidate new ecological authority, not a renderer constant. If the campaign rejects it, the rejection must be preserved.

### Budget-selection rule

Founder count, initial food and ongoing food production must be chosen explicitly rather than by arbitrary doubling.

The first implementation should use area-aware values as a starting hypothesis and test nearby bounded alternatives. The old world area is 630,000; the candidate area is 1,080,000, an area ratio of ~1.7143.

A reasonable initial hypothesis is therefore approximately:

- founders: **58–60** rather than 34;
- initial food: **245–250** rather than 145;
- ongoing food rate: **~17/s** rather than 10/s in the current product configuration.

These are experimental starting values, not promoted constants. Qualification must justify the final chosen budget.

### Population cap

The existing cap of 260 remains initially.

M4a must not raise the cap merely to make the screen look busier. A higher cap requires evidence that the ecological model is naturally pressing against 260 in representative runs and that additional headroom improves the living-world question without making x40 unusable.

### Resource-regime semantics

Dense vs diffuse habitats must differ in a small number of interpretable spatial properties, initially resource dispersion/radius.

The first M4a implementation should avoid coupling habitat identity to unrelated hidden bonuses. The goal is to create different search/foraging problems, not to assign arbitrary fitness multipliers.

Food kind and habitat regime are separate axes:

- diet axis: food A vs food B;
- search axis: dense vs diffuse.

This produces at least four ecological combinations before adding any new trait dimension.

### Stability in the first M4a epoch

The first qualified mosaic should use stable habitat locations. Existing evidence shows that the old moving-niche configuration can destroy population/diversity.

Temporal habitat motion, weather and disturbance remain later hypotheses. They must not be bundled into the first M4a causal change.

## M4a qualification

M4a is not successful merely because one seed looks attractive.

### A. Long-horizon campaign

Minimum qualification:

- at least **8 deterministic seeds** at 1,800 simulated seconds;
- at least **3 of those seeds** extended to 3,600 simulated seconds;
- fixed-step exact execution;
- lifecycle witness attached by apparatus for lineage metrics;
- same metric definitions as the living-world audit unless a correction is explicitly documented before results are inspected.

### B. Spatial occupation

At 1,800 s, target median occupied fraction on the existing normalized 12x9 world grid:

**>= 0.25**

A result below 0.20 is a failure of the world-occupation goal even if population is high.

The metric is normalized to logical geometry; larger pixels alone cannot satisfy it.

### C. Strategy persistence

At 1,800 s:

- median effective coarse strategy count target: **>= 10**;
- median 1,800s / 600s effective-strategy retention ratio target: **>= 0.65**.

Baseline retention is approximately 5.86 / 13.52 = 0.43.

A candidate that shows spectacular early variation and then collapses by 1,800 s does not pass.

### D. Lineage persistence

At 1,800 s:

- median active founder roots target: **>= 3**;
- median effective lineage count target: **>= 2.2**;
- no more than 2/8 seeds should collapse to a single founder lineage.

This is not a requirement to preserve all founders. Selection remains allowed. The gate exists to reject worlds whose apparent phenotypic variety is repeatedly carried by one winning lineage.

### E. Local adaptation / ecological differentiation

The dense-vs-diffuse effect must be replicated on the final candidate with a paired uniform-regime control.

Across at least 8 seeds, the final candidate should satisfy all of:

- diffuse-minus-dense speed median >= **+0.20**;
- diffuse-minus-dense sense median >= **+5**;
- diffuse-minus-dense size median <= **-0.60**;
- at least 6/8 seeds have the expected sign for speed, sense and size simultaneously.

The heterogeneous-minus-uniform contrast must remain materially larger than spatial sorting in the control.

This gate may fail without invalidating the whole project; failure means the first mosaic hypothesis is insufficient and must be redesigned before Owner delivery.

### F. No hidden population-only pass

Population is characterization, not a primary success metric.

A candidate with far more organisms but unchanged/poorer strategy and lineage persistence does not pass.

### G. x40 usability

Owner has explicitly rejected future broad testing without useful fast-forward.

The composite M4a browser build must retain:

- target 40x control;
- separate achieved-speed reporting;
- no historical catch-up debt;
- pause/visibility semantics from the x40 contract;
- fixed `1/60` steps.

Representative natural M4a worlds should target median achieved speed **>= 35x** on the qualification runner/browser, with no ordinary qualification seed below **30x**.

If ecology causes lower performance, first profile the real bottleneck. Do not simplify biology or falsify the achieved-speed display merely to pass this gate.

A final Owner candidate should preferably return to approximately >=38x before delivery if bounded optimization can do so without semantic distortion.

### H. Protected regressions

M4a must rerun and preserve all still-relevant protected infrastructure:

- deterministic RNG implementation/topology except where an intentional M4a RNG call is explicitly accounted for;
- M2a witness passivity;
- M3 world/view separation as a boundary principle;
- DPR/pointer mapping behavior;
- x40 fixed-step/debt/visibility semantics;
- standalone deterministic build.

Because M4a intentionally changes world ecology, old V1/M1 final-state hashes are not expected for M4a runs. Protected tests must distinguish process invariants from intentionally divergent biological outcomes.

### I. Intentional divergence receipt

Before promotion, evidence must explicitly list every new causal parameter and every new RNG call site introduced by M4a.

No new source of randomness may hide inside browser presentation code.

## M4b — Phenotype Legibility

M4b is presentation-only and should be developed only after M4a exposes meaningful biological differences in headless evidence.

### Goal

A viewer should be able to notice several inherited differences while watching the world, without opening aggregate statistics.

### Initial mapping principles

Use continuous visual mappings rather than invented discrete species labels.

At minimum, the browser should make these existing traits materially legible:

- **diet** — primary color family remains a valid channel;
- **size** — body size/silhouette;
- **speed** — motion/tail or another motion-linked visual cue;
- **sense** — sensor-ring radius or equivalent;
- at least one of **efficiency / wander / inherited hue** through a secondary non-conflicting cue if visual testing shows it remains readable.

Inherited `hue` should not silently imply diet if diet already owns the primary body-color semantics. If used, it should become a secondary lineage/individual accent rather than overwrite ecological meaning.

### No fake biological information

M4b must not invent labels such as predator, scout or species unless the underlying model actually supports those concepts.

Visuals may reveal phenotype; they must not narrate unsupported biology.

### Causal parity

For the same M4a state and input sequence, enabling/disabling M4b presentation must produce exact equality of:

- authoritative simulation state;
- RNG state/call topology;
- lifecycle witness sequence.

No rendering property may feed back into simulation.

### Readability qualification

Machine checks should verify mapping monotonicity/extremes and browser correctness, but visual success ultimately requires Owner judgement.

The Owner candidate should deliberately include organisms near contrasting trait ranges so that readability can be judged without waiting for a rare evolutionary accident.

## Composite Owner gate

Do **not** ask the Owner to broadly test intermediate M4a/M4b builds unless a focused diagnostic is genuinely necessary.

The next intended broad Owner candidate should combine:

- repaired M3 presentation semantics;
- qualified honest x40;
- machine-qualified M4a ecological mosaic;
- machine-qualified M4b phenotype legibility.

That one test can also complete any still-deferred M3/x40 product evidence.

Owner success criteria are qualitative but specific:

- the world no longer feels mostly empty;
- several local populations/habitats are visually apparent;
- organisms differ enough that following particular phenotypes is worthwhile;
- x40 exposes meaningful ecological/evolutionary change rather than merely fast-forwarding through the same two archetypes;
- resize/mobile-like presentation remains acceptable;
- speed changes and controls remain responsive;
- the world feels materially more alive, not merely busier.

A machine PASS cannot substitute for a negative Owner judgement on those product criteria.

## Explicitly deferred beyond M4

Unless M4 evidence fails in a way that specifically requires them, do not bundle:

- predation;
- direct organism combat;
- flocking/social systems;
- mating choice;
- neural-network/behavior-policy genomes;
- weather;
- terrain physics;
- destructive environment;
- retained M2b lineage database;
- replay/ghost tooling;
- arbitrary new genome dimensions.

The next question after M4 should be chosen from observed limitations of the richer world, not from a predetermined feature wishlist.

## Promotion rule

M4a and M4b may be tested as one product but must remain separately attributable.

If M4b is good and M4a is biologically weak, presentation polish must not hide the failed ecology.

If M4a is biologically strong and M4b is ugly, the ecology remains valuable evidence but the composite product is not ready for Owner promotion.

Only positive long-horizon evidence plus positive Owner product judgement can promote the combined direction.
