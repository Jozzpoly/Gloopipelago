## Allowed scope

M4b may change only deterministic browser presentation and truthful explanatory copy:

- organism glyph/silhouette;
- visual mapping of already-existing traits;
- habitat field rendering;
- sensor visualization;
- layer/opacity/contrast choices;
- presentation-only helpers needed to make those mappings explicit and testable.

It must not change biology, food placement, habitat semantics, mutation, reproduction, metabolism, RNG, lifecycle events, world geometry, controls, interventions or x40 execution policy.

## Information hierarchy

Do not encode seven genes as seven equally loud decorations.

- **diet** owns the primary body color family;
- **size** owns primary silhouette scale;
- **speed** gets a monotonic motion/elongation/trailing magnitude cue;
- **sense** gets a monotonic compact sensing cue, replacing screen-filling full circles;
- inherited **hue** may become a small secondary identity accent that never replaces diet color.

`eff` and `wander` remain available in statistics unless a genuinely clean secondary channel emerges. M4b is not required to create visual noise merely to display every variable.

## Organism glyph constraints

- organic, cheap silhouette rather than a generic circle;
- orientation may follow actual velocity, with deterministic fallback to existing body angle;
- greater `size` must produce a visibly larger primary silhouette;
- greater inherited `speed` must produce a stronger speed cue;
- greater `sense` must produce a larger sense cue without restoring giant complete rings;
- diet endpoints remain clearly distinct;
- inherited hue accent changes with `hue` but remains secondary;
- energy may affect a small transient brightness/core cue, but must not masquerade as inherited phenotype;
- no fake labels such as species, scout, grazer or predator.

## Habitat rendering constraints

Replace hard debug rectangles with a deterministic soft field derived only from authoritative habitat `x/y/spread/kind/regime`.

Requirements:

- no RNG or random texture in draw code;
- A/B remains visually related to food colors;
- dense/diffuse is perceptibly different but not represented as walls/collision;
- habitat visuals do not overpower organisms;
- geometry remains faithful to the authoritative habitat centre/spread and never feeds back into simulation.
