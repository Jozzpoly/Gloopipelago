## Minimum required smoke

1. Start the exact candidate and let the world run normally at 1x for a short moment.
2. Resize the same live browser substantially from wide to narrow/tall or mobile-like, then back wide. Confirm the same living world continues rather than resetting or becoming causally compressed/stretched.
3. While narrow/mobile-like, judge whether the complete-world `contain` presentation is still usable and legible enough. This check is required by the promoted M3 contract.
4. After the resize sequence, use `Ten sam seed` once and `Nowy seed` once. Confirm both restart as complete correctly framed worlds.
5. Exercise a world click and, if convenient, a visible letterbox/pillarbox click. In-world click should add food; outside-world bands should remain non-causal.
6. Select `4x`, then `10x`, `20x`, and `40x`. At 40x, check that the product remains responsive enough to pause, change speed, add food and trigger catastrophe.
7. Watch `cel` versus `realnie`. On an ordinary world, real speed should normally stay near the selected target. It is acceptable for heavy worlds to underachieve if `realnie` tells the truth and controls remain responsive.
8. After running at 40x, switch directly to 1x. Confirm the world visibly returns to normal speed rather than continuing to race because of old fast-forward debt.
9. Pause at high speed. The world should stop promptly.
10. Report any visual discontinuity, unexpected reset, input lag, stale/implausible `realnie` value, browser warning/error, or behavior that simply feels wrong.

A recording is particularly useful if there is any issue, because it lets the machine evidence be compared against exact product behavior.
