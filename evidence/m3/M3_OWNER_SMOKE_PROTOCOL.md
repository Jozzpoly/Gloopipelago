## Purpose

Machine qualification has established the bounded causal/controller contract. Owner smoke answers the remaining product question: does the fixed 900x700 logical world remain usable, legible and natural enough when presented through arbitrary real browser viewports?

This is especially important because M3 intentionally chooses full-world aspect-preserving `contain` rather than stretching or cropping.

## Minimum smoke

1. Open the candidate in a normal desktop browser at a comfortably wide window. Confirm the full world is visible and behaves normally.
2. While the same world is running, resize the browser substantially narrower/taller and then wide again. The organisms/world history should continue; resizing must feel visual rather than like resetting/compressing the world.
3. In a viewport that visibly has letterbox/pillarbox space, click once clearly inside a band. It should add **no food**. Click inside the actual world; pointer food should work normally.
4. After resizing, use **Ten sam seed** and **Nowy seed**. Both should start a complete, correctly framed world; reset should preserve the seed, new seed should change it.
5. Exercise Pause/Wznów, +50 food, Katastrofa and the speed slider. Leave 4x running long enough to judge whether the already-established 4x workflow still feels healthy and whether world time advances approximately four times real time.
6. If convenient, also inspect a narrow/mobile-like viewport. The key question is not whether it fills every pixel, but whether the complete world is still readable and the unused bands are acceptable rather than awkward or broken.

## Owner evidence

A short textual verdict is sufficient if nothing suspicious happens. A recording is more valuable if presentation, resize behavior, pointer mapping, responsiveness or 4x performance looks questionable.

Useful verdict shape:

- `PASS` — presentation/interaction is acceptable and no blocking regression noticed; or
- describe what looked wrong, especially viewport shape, bands, pointer location, resizing, visual scale, responsiveness or 4x behavior.

## Promotion boundary

A positive Owner smoke does not authorize unrelated work. It closes only the real-browser product gate for this exact M3 candidate. Final promotion still requires recording the Owner receipt and verifying that subsequent evidence-only commits did not alter the qualified runtime artifact.
