# Gloopipelago — Genome / Embodiment / Co-evolution Redesign Audit Plan

**Date:** 2026-09-10  
**Status:** PREDECLARED ANALYSIS PLAN / NO NEW BIOLOGY IMPLEMENTATION YET

## Trigger

The exact M4 composite received Owner PRODUCT FAIL after a broad real-browser test. The world is technically healthier and somewhat richer than earlier versions, but organisms remain behaviorally shallow. Existing inherited variation mostly changes scalar parameters inside one shared foraging controller.

The next task is not M4 tuning. It is to recover and test the deeper slim-genome idea.

## Core question

How can a **small inherited genome** generate a substantially larger space of observable ways of living while remaining deterministic, computationally tractable at x40, experimentally inspectable, and capable of reciprocal organism<->environment evolution?

## Intended conceptual chain to recover

`genotype`
`-> developmental / phenotypic expression`
`-> embodied capacities and costs`
`-> perception`
`-> lifetime internal state / learning / plasticity`
`-> action selection`
`-> local persistent environmental effects`
`-> altered ecological opportunities and pressures`
`-> selection and co-evolution`

Each arrow is an independent design obligation. A gene list is not sufficient evidence that the chain exists.

## Five-layer separation

Candidate architectures must keep these concepts separate enough to reason about and instrument:

1. **Inherited genotype** — compact heritable parameters/rules.
2. **Expressed phenotype/body** — morphology, physiology, sensors/effectors and costs derived from genotype/development.
3. **Lifetime state** — energy plus acquired memory, internal drives, adaptation/plasticity; normally not inherited as world knowledge.
4. **Action policy** — how perception + phenotype + lifetime state produce behavior.
5. **Environment state** — persistent local fields/materials/resources/traces that organisms can alter and later encounter.

## Redesign hypotheses to compare rather than assume

### H1 — richer scalar genome only

Add more direct genes/actions while retaining a mostly hand-authored controller.

Expected advantage: cheap and deterministic.

Primary falsification risk: still maps many numbers into one archetypal policy and recreates M4 at higher dimensionality.

### H2 — compact regulatory / developmental genotype

A small set of genes controls interacting expression rules, producing coupled morphology/metabolism/sensing/secretions rather than direct independent stats.

Expected advantage: nonlinear genotype->phenotype map can create correlated body plans and trade-offs from few genes.

Primary risk: complexity becomes opaque or merely decorative if expression does not alter ecological affordances.

### H3 — evolvable compact controller

Phenotype includes a small deterministic action-selection network/state machine whose weights/thresholds/topology are heritable or developmentally derived.

Expected advantage: organisms can evolve different policies, not merely different capacities.

Primary risks: uninspectable behavior, unstable search space, high computational cost, or pseudo-intelligence with no meaningful environment.

### H4 — embodied affordance composition

The genome controls a small collection of physical/functional modules (movement, sensing, feeding, storage, secretion, adhesion, digestion, etc.); behavior emerges from what the body can do plus a compact controller.

Expected advantage: different strategies become grounded in real capacities and costs.

Primary risk: module catalogue becomes a disguised feature list or hand-authored species taxonomy.

### H5 — environment-first niche construction

Keep organism policy relatively compact but give organisms several metabolically coupled ways to alter persistent local environment state, allowing cross-feeding, trails, depletion/regeneration, residues or material changes.

Expected advantage: ecological feedback creates new opportunities without requiring a huge brain/genome.

Primary risk: static behavior merely paints fields; environmental state adds visual complexity without reciprocal selection.

The likely long-term design may combine parts of H2-H5, but the audit must not assume the combination before isolated probes.

## Required evidence before choosing architecture

A candidate mechanism should demonstrate at least several of these properties in bounded probes:

- same small genome length can produce strongly different observable life histories;
- perturbing one gene can have coupled, interpretable downstream phenotype effects rather than only one slider;
- organisms choose different actions in the same external situation because of phenotype/lifetime state, not hard-coded species labels;
- lifetime-acquired state changes future behavior and resets on birth unless explicitly encoded as inherited capacity;
- organisms create persistent local environmental changes that survive their departure/death for a meaningful interval;
- another organism can benefit, avoid, exploit or otherwise respond to those environmental changes;
- those feedbacks alter reproductive success across generations;
- multiple strategies can coexist or replace one another for mechanistic reasons visible in the causal record;
- x40 remains plausible on natural populations or there is a clearly isolated performance optimization path;
- behavior is inspectable enough to explain why a lineage is succeeding.

## Product evidence

Numerical diversity metrics are necessary but insufficient. A later Owner build must visibly exhibit at least two or three **qualitatively different ways of living** without needing a stats panel to explain them.

Examples of qualitative distinction (not commitments): one lineage may exploit a locally produced residue; another may range widely; another may remain around self-modified resource patches. The mechanism must emerge from the same general rules, not from hidden class labels.

## Negative constraints

Do not:

- repair M4 by simply adding more scalar genes;
- add named species/classes;
- inherit acquired map coordinates or other lifetime knowledge directly;
- add a large neural network because 'AI behavior' sounds richer;
- add predation/mating/sociality merely as spectacle;
- add persistent environment fields that have no causal effect on organism fitness;
- optimize for a single diversity score;
- sacrifice determinism/provenance unnecessarily;
- change the frozen historical core or current qualified donor branches during this audit.

## Audit sequence

1. Recover original project intent and compare it with exact M4 implementation.
2. Perform literature/donor research on compact genotype-to-phenotype maps, embodied ALife, plasticity, stigmergy/niche construction and evolvable controllers.
3. Build a missing-link ledger for current M4.
4. Design at least three **minimal competing probes** rather than one giant redesign.
5. Predeclare success/failure signals for each probe.
6. Run headless experiments before browser integration.
7. Choose or reject mechanisms based on causal evidence and Owner-facing qualitative potential.
8. Only then contract the next biological epoch.

## Stop condition

The audit is not complete when it has generated many ideas. It is complete when we can state, with evidence, **which minimal mechanisms are worth combining into the next organism/world model and which attractive ideas should be rejected or deferred.**
