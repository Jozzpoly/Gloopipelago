## Owner evidence

The Owner supplied an approximately 6m38s silent desktop recording covering varied natural interaction/testing, including long high-speed observation and multiple world states/seeds, followed by explicit qualitative feedback.

The Owner judged the candidate only **minimally better** than the previously rejected product and still fundamentally too raw and boring.

The decisive complaint was not a mechanics bug or presentation regression. It was that the organisms still behave like a very shallow first-generation ecosystem simulation: the apparent genome largely collapses to a short list of scalar parameters such as speed, sight/sense, size, efficiency and diet specialization, while the organisms continue to execute essentially the same life policy.

The Owner explicitly called out the loss of the earlier slim-genome / glut direction in which organisms and environment were expected to change together and co-evolve.

## Video-level product observation

Cross-sectional review of the recording confirms the qualitative complaint. Across the run, the visible state changes in population size, local clustering, phenotype proportions and resource occupancy, but the repertoire of organism-environment interactions remains narrow:

- forage for currently digestible resource;
- move toward it or wander;
- consume;
- metabolize;
- reproduce;
- die.

The richer M4 spatial mosaic creates multiple local populations and the M4b renderer makes phenotype variation more visible, but neither layer creates new classes of organism action, persistent acquired state, environmental construction or reciprocal organism<->environment history.

The result is more differentiated **parameter variation inside one behavioral archetype**, not a materially richer space of ways of life.

## Why the machine evidence is still valid

This Owner FAIL does not invalidate the technical findings already established:

- repaired world/view and DPR semantics remain qualified;
- honest x40 execution remains qualified;
- M4a measurably improves occupancy, lineage persistence, strategy-proxy diversity and local adaptation relative to the older baseline;
- M4b remains presentation-only and causally neutral;
- M4b v2 is visually clearer than M4a and the rejected M4b v1.

Those are bounded technical claims. They are simply insufficient for the product goal.

A machine PASS cannot override a negative Owner judgement about whether the living world is interesting to observe.

## Root problem to investigate next

Current-best diagnosis: **the genotype-to-life map has low expressive rank.**

The maintained organism controller is shared by every organism. Genes modulate scalar quantities inside that controller, but do not substantially alter the organism's action repertoire, body/environment coupling, acquired lifetime state or capacity to construct niches.

Therefore a larger numerical genome or stronger mutation alone is unlikely to solve the problem. A thousand scalar variants can still map to the same basic life loop.

The next work must recover the earlier project intent:

`small inherited genotype -> developmental/phenotypic expression -> embodied capacities and trade-offs -> lifetime-acquired state -> behavior -> persistent environmental modification -> changed ecological opportunity/selection -> evolution`

The exact architecture is **not yet chosen**. This receipt authorizes a skeptical redesign/research phase, not immediate implementation of a new giant genome.
